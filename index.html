Approach

How I build reliable workflows

The goal is output people can trust, so every step has a check and every change can be traced.

  1. Define the phasesWrite down the steps and their order, then enforce that order in code.
  2. Automate the checksTest for accuracy, coverage and validity, and measure with evaluation sets.
  3. Keep people in the loopPut review gates where judgement matters and make the next step clear.
  4. Version and improveKeep everything in Git, document it, and feed findings back into the source.
